# Gnojo Brand Foundation

## Name

**Gnojo** (pronounced **NO-joe**) combines *gnosis*—knowledge gained through understanding—with *dojo*—a place for disciplined practice and improvement.

Gnojo turns technical knowledge into guided action. It helps people navigate problems, practice sound troubleshooting, preserve expertise, and improve how support work gets done.

## Positioning

Gnojo is an AI-assisted technical knowledge and troubleshooting platform for the people who keep technology running.

It combines structured knowledge, guided workflows, simulation, validation, and carefully controlled AI assistance. Unlike a general-purpose chatbot, Gnojo makes troubleshooting logic visible, reviewable, testable, and reusable.

## Tagline

**Navigate. Diagnose. Resolve.**

## Brand promise

Knowledge becomes more valuable when people can apply it confidently.

## Personality

- Calm under pressure
- Precise without being cold
- Capable without being showy
- Instructional without being patronizing
- Honest about uncertainty
- Focused on action and learning

## Writing principles

- Use direct, plain language.
- Explain what matters at the moment it matters.
- Prefer useful next steps over abstract commentary.
- Preserve technical accuracy when simplifying.
- Never imply that AI output is authoritative without review.
- Help users understand, not merely complete, the task.

## Visual direction

The primary identity is the **Open Gate** mark: an unmistakable geometric **G** containing a forward-facing doorway cut in its negative space. It represents entering a place of knowledge and leaving more capable. The established navy-and-blue palette communicates trust, clarity, and technical confidence while preserving continuity with the product users already know.

The Open Gate mark is the sole product symbol. Gnojo does not use separate feature or module logos.

The interface should remain calm, structured, and workspace-oriented in both light and dark themes.

## Data continuity

The Gnojo rebrand does not rename existing workflow IDs, publication IDs, storage folders, or historical snapshots. Brand identity changes independently from persisted technical identifiers so existing data remains stable.
